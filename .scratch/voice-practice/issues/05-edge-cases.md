# Decide what a lesson pass means when the learner jumps around
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02

## Question

How do seek, A-B repeat, speed changes, wheel skips (±5 s), pausing in the middle of a speaking pause and switching lessons affect Attempts? Settle what the "latest pass" of a lesson is, when a new Attempt replaces an old one for the same pause, and what is kept or thrown away when a pause is only partly heard.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-11-21 with the user (HITL grilling, two rounds, all recommendations accepted).

- **No "lesson pass".** Each speaking pause keeps only its **newest Attempt**; a new one replaces it, pauses not answered again keep their old one. A-B repeat, seeks and listening again all follow from this, so one lesson can hold Attempts from different days.
- **Partly heard pause**: an Attempt is kept only if the pause's whole kept window (+0.2 s to −0.1 s) played without a seek. A seek, a ±5 s skip (screen or media buttons) or a lesson change inside the window discards it, and the old Attempt stays.
- **Silence**: an Attempt with no voice in it is never saved and never replaces an older one.
- **Pausing inside a speaking pause** (Pimsleur's "pause if you need more time"): the mic keeps listening for up to **10 s** while the lesson is paused; the Attempt is the part heard plus the paused time. Only a seek or a lesson change counts as an interruption.
- **Speed**: Attempts are recorded at any speed; pauses are measured in lesson time, and the speaker's line plays back at the current speed.
- **The 5 kept lessons** are the 5 with the most recently recorded Attempts; recording in a 6th deletes every Attempt of the least recently recorded one. Opening or listening without recording doesn't count.
- **No age shown**: "▶ Me" doesn't say when an Attempt was recorded.
- **Deleting**: turning "Record my answers" off only stops new recording; existing Attempts stay playable. Settings gets a **"Delete my answers"** button that deletes all Attempts after a confirmation.
