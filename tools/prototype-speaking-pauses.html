Which silences in lesson I_05 are speaking pauses?

The app will keep the learner's voice only during speaking pauses, the silences where you answer out loud. The only thing it has to go on is the transcript timings. This page applies one rule to every silence in I_05 and lets you listen to each one so you can say where it is wrong. Change the rule at the top, and the list and counts update. Each silence is sorted by what comes before and after it (Russian or English, told apart by the alphabet, because Whisper's own language tags are often wrong). The outlined line is the speaker's line your Attempt would be compared with.
